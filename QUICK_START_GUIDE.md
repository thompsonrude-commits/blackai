# 9jai Quick Start Guide

**App URL:** https://9jai.web.app

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Login
1. Open https://9jai.web.app
2. Click "Sign Up" to create a new account
3. Enter your email and password (minimum 6 characters)
4. Click "Create Account"
5. You're now logged in!

### Step 2: Choose a Language
1. Click "Languages" in the sidebar
2. Select any Nigerian language (Edo, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, etc.)
3. Start chatting with the AI

### Step 3: Chat
1. Type your question or message
2. Press Enter or click Send button
3. AI responds with helpful information
4. Your chat is automatically saved

## Tips & Tricks

### Get Better Responses
- **Be specific:** "Write a Python function to sort an array" (not "write code")
- **Provide context:** "I'm learning Edo language"
- **Ask follow-ups:** "Can you explain that differently?"
- **Request examples:** "Show me an example"

### Use the Library
1. Click "Library" button in sidebar
2. Search for past conversations
3. Click to view full conversation
4. Export or delete as needed

### Voice Mode
1. Click "Voice Mode" button in header
2. Click microphone to record
3. AI transcribes and responds automatically
4. Click "Voice Mode" again to turn off

### Image Generation
- Ask AI: "Generate an image of..."
- AI creates images from text descriptions
- Download or share generated images

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Login
- Check email is correct
- Password must be at least 6 characters
- Try signing up instead of signing in

### Chat Not Saving
- Check internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Try a different browser

### Image Generation Not Working
- Check internet connection
- Try a simpler description
- Wait a few seconds and try again

### Sidebar Not Showing
- Wait 1 minute of inactivity
- Sidebar will appear every 10 seconds
- Move mouse or click to hide it again

## Privacy & Security

### Your Data
- **Saved Locally:** Chat history saved in your browser
- **Secure:** Uses HTTPS encryption
- **Private:** Only you can see your chats
- **Controlled:** You can delete anytime

### Clearing Data
1. Open browser settings
2. Go to Privacy/Security
3. Clear browsing data
4. Select "Cookies and site data"
5. Clear data for 9jai.web.app
