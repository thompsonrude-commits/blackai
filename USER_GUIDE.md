## 🎨 Image Generation

### How to Generate Images

#### Method 1: Natural Description
Just describe what you want to see:
- "a lion"
- "a beautiful sunset"
- "a majestic eagle"
- "Nigerian skyline"
- "the Aso Rock"

#### Method 2: Explicit Request
Use clear generation commands:
- "generate image of a lion hunting"
- "create image of Lagos at night"
- "draw me a traditional African mask"
- "paint a beautiful landscape"

#### Method 3: Visual Design
Request specific visual content:
- "logo for my tech startup"
- "poster for music event"
- "banner for website"
- "profile picture of a professional"

### What Works Best
✅ Short, descriptive phrases (1-6 words)
✅ Visual elements: colors, subjects, scenes
✅ Natural language: "a red car", "the sunset over ocean"
✅ Specific requests: "realistic portrait", "cartoon style"

### What Doesn't Trigger Images
❌ Questions: "what is a lion", "how does a lion look"
❌ Document requests: "generate a report", "create a list"
❌ Code requests: "write a function", "make a script"
❌ General chat: "tell me about lions", "explain sunsets"

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Image Not Generating?
1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Try rephrasing your request
4. Clear browser cache
5. Try a different browser

### Chat Not Responding?
1. Check network connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Clear browser cache
4. Check if you hit rate limit (wait 1 minute)

### Slow Performance?
1. Close unnecessary browser tabs
2. Clear cache and cookies
3. Use a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari)
4. Check your internet speed
