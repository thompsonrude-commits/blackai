## 💬 Chat Features

### Session Persistence (Chat History)

**What it does:**
- Automatically saves all your conversations
- Restores your chat when you return to the app
- Keeps you on the same language page after refresh

**How to use:**
1. Start a conversation in any language
2. Your messages are automatically saved
3. Close the app or refresh the page
4. Your chat history and active page are restored

**Example:**
- You're learning Edo language and have a 20-message conversation
- You close the browser
- When you return, you see the same Edo page with all 20 messages

## 🎨 Image Generator

### Create Images from Text

**How to use:**
1. Click the **"Create"** button at the bottom of the chat
2. The Image Generator modal opens
3. Describe the image you want in detail
4. Click **"Generate Image"**
5. Wait for the AI to create your image

### Write Good Prompts

**Good prompts include:**
- What you want to see
- Style (e.g., "oil painting", "photograph", "cartoon")
- Mood or atmosphere
- Colors and lighting
- Level of detail

**Example prompts:**
```
"A beautiful sunset over mountains, oil painting style, warm colors, highly detailed"
"A futuristic city with flying cars, neon lights, cyberpunk style, night time"
"A cozy coffee shop interior, warm lighting, watercolor painting style"
```

### Download & Share

**After generating an image:**
- **Download:** Save to your device
- **Share:** Share via native share or copy URL
- **View History:** See all previously generated images

### Image History

1. Click **"View History"** to see past generations
2. Click any image to view it again
3. Download or share from history

## 🔄 Session Management

### Your Chat Library

All your conversations are saved in your browser's local storage.

**What's saved:**
- All messages (yours and the AI's)
- Timestamps
- Language and topic
- Session title and date

**How to access:**
- Your sessions are automatically restored when you return
- Each language has its own chat history
- Switching languages shows that language's chat history

### Switching Languages

1. Click on a different language in the sidebar
2. Your previous chat for that language is restored
3. Or start a new conversation

### Clearing History

To clear all saved conversations:
1. Open browser developer tools (F12)
2. Go to Application → Local Storage
3. Find entries starting with "user_sessions"
4. Delete them

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Chat History Not Saving

**Problem:** Messages disappear after refresh

**Solution:**
1. Check if localStorage is enabled in browser
2. Clear browser cache and try again
3. Try a different browser

### Image Generation Not Working

**Problem:** Images won't generate

**Solution:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Try a simpler prompt
3. Wait a few seconds and try again
4. Fallback images will display if API is unavailable

### Education Hub Not Loading

**Problem:** Learning materials won't display

**Solution:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser

### AI Not Responding

**Problem:** Chat seems stuck

**Solution:**
1. Click the **stop button** (red square)
2. Try a simpler question
3. Refresh the page
